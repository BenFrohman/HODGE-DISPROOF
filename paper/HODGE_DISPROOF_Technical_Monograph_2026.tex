\begin{abstract}
Standalone 31-module Lean 4 formalization (`Hodge/*.lean`, `Main.lean`, `EVIDENCE\_LEDGER.md`) specifying the exact dependent type-theoretic signature (D\_bad, gamma\_bad, forall z, cl(z) = gamma\_bad -> False) required for any putative counterexample to the rational Hodge Conjecture, together with automated audit checks tracking which candidate geometric inputs are inhabited vs. open.
\end{abstract}

\section{Scope, Scientific Honesty, and Non-Claim Boundary}
Formalizes the type-theoretic verification schema and audit ledger for testing candidate counterexamples in Lean 4; explicitly records Clay status as OPEN and does not claim an inhabited unconditional disproof.
