# Thom–Sebastiani on the Lin–Zhang family

**Author:** Benjamin Stanley Frohman (@BenFrohman)
**License:** Apache-2.0
**Clay status:** open in general

Lin–Zhang generate the identity-sector Hodge lattice by
`E_I = L_0 ⊔ ⋯ ⊔ L_m` from rank-one factorizations of the binary
blocks. That is the *positive* generator set on their family.

It is not formalized here. It does not inhabit a miss on a host
outside the family.

Do not replace `Hodge/PrimitiveDecomposition.lean` with a Scheme pack.
Those are Lefschetz identities.

Hodge number `h^{2,2}` is not `Δ_Hdg`. For `m=2`, `d=8` the paper’s
primitive *rational* middle rank is `(d-1)^{m+1} = 343`.
