**Date:** 24 September 2026

| # | Type | What would fill it | What does not fill it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fourfold partner `Y ≠ X = V(F)` with `ω_Y ≃ O_Y` | a distinct Calabi–Yau fourfold | `S`, `Bl_Π X`, `HMF^gr(F)`, a residual quintic |
| 2 | Kernel `Ε ∈ D^b(X × Y)` with `Φ_Ε` an equivalence | a Fourier–Mukai kernel | a list of ideals `I(Z_i)` |
| 3 | `ExistsNontrivial92` | `T : Fin 92 → Block` closing with `n_1 > 1` | `(1,1,1)` |
| 4 | `RemainingLemma` / Collatz | `∀ n > 0 ∃ k, T^[k] n ∈ {1,2,4}` | `rfl` on the name |
| 5 | `rational_hodge_conjecture_is_false` | `⟨X, γ, miss⟩` with `γ ∉ im(cl_X)` | `(V(F), [Π])` or `[S]` |

On a locked host, `[Π]` and `[S]` are cycle classes, so they lie in `im(cl_X)`.
Integral Hodge counterexamples inhabit a different sentence.

All five stay unwritten.
